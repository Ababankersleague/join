Tournament Modalities & Rules

1. Eligibility:

Only confirmed staff members of registered banks in Aba are eligible to play. No "mercenaries" or external players allowed.

2. Squad Composition:

Each team is allowed a maximum of 22 registered players. Only players with a valid, system-generated ID card and staff ID verification are permitted on the field.

3. Verification:

All players must undergo physical verification on match days using the QR code on their generated ID cards.

4. Discipline:

Yellow and Red card fines apply. Any form of violent conduct will lead to immediate disqualification of the player and a potential team ban.
